- A tie take through `POST /api/v1/takes` now compares each tie line with the source in `expect`, and, with `affected`, the Affected destinations that the caller saw. Sode compares every level, and `expect` takes a list with one source for each level for a mixed tie line. If a tie line moved, Sode refuses the whole take with 409 `tie_line_changed` and sends nothing. The answer gives each tie line as it is now. Sode locks the tie lines of a take until it ends. A take to the upstream destination of a tie line waits for that lock.
