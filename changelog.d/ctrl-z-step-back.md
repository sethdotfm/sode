- Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) on Home now goes one take further back with each press, as the panel Undo cell does. It undoes only takes from this browser tab and passes over takes that an undo already put back. Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd+Shift+Z) redoes what Ctrl+Z undid, one take at a time. A new take clears that redo list. The Palette has a Redo row, and the key list shows both keys.
