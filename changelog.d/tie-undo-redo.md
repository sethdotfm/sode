- Undo of a tie take puts back only the destination asked for and never switches a tie line back by itself. Sometimes the destination would not get its source from before, because this take switched a tie line that still carries what it set. Then the Undo confirm offers to put that tie line back and lists its Affected destinations. If something else changed, Undo sends nothing, says what changed, and offers the Tie line picker. Redo of a tie take opens the picker. It chooses the tie lines of the take if they cost no more than the cheapest, and marks them "Used last time". Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z work on tie takes sent from the picker. An undone tie row says that its tie lines are left as they are. The API and the panel Undo cell refuse such an Undo with 409 `tie_line_changed`.
