- A tie take through `POST /api/v1/takes` now runs the Protect check on every hop before it sends anything. A Protect or a Router lock on a hop refuses that route, and none of its hops go. The other routes of the take go. Tie routes are ordinary connects, also in `fireMode` native. The `result` of a tie route comes from all its hops: a route whose last hop was already, after a hop that changed a tie line, is `connected`.
