- A tie take through `POST /api/v1/takes` now runs the Protect check on every hop before it sends anything. A Protect or a Router lock on a hop refuses that route, and none of its hops go. The other routes of the take go. Tie routes are ordinary connects, also in `fireMode` native. The `result` of a tie route comes from all its hops. If a hop changed a tie line, the route is `connected`, also when the last hop was already.
