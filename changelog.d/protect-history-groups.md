- History: a Protect or Free Protect on many destinations is one Group row. This includes the Protect list, the Grid popup's shield on picked names and Free Sode's Protects. The row shows the chip, the number of destinations and its status, with "2/32 failed" when some fail. Open it to see each destination. Undo all reverses each destination that is still as the Group left it, and the Confirm tells how many it skips.
- History: Protects that another system sets or frees in one burst show as one Group for each kind and direction.
- History: Protect actions by the same person in the same direction fold into one row. Each must come within the stack gap of the one before (Settings, "Stack quick changes", default 30 seconds). A Protect and a Free Protect do not fold together, and an undo stays apart.
- Settings > This browser: "Stack quick changes to one destination" is now "Stack quick changes in History", since it also folds quick Protect actions.
