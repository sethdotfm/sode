- The Palette does the action of a Settings button. A button that opens a dialog opens it where you are, with no Settings view. Examples are Change my PIN, My API keys, My profile and Export & Import. Some buttons open Settings at the button and press it, since what they do shows there. These are Clear History, Clear Sode's Protects, Manage users, Apply an Access preset, Run setup again and Save as my default. Each button keeps its own checks, such as a PIN or a confirm.
- Sign out and Step out are Palette actions. Sign in, Sign out, Step out and Lock also work on the Communication Log and the panel pages. The Palette shows each action one time: a search for "sign in" shows one Sign in.
- The Palette puts the rows you use most first among equal matches, and Sign in, Sign out, Lock, Settings and Home before other equal matches. A category guess from letters in order no longer wins over a row name: "sig" finds Sign in or Sign out, not Settings.
