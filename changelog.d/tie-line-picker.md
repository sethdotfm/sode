- The main page opens the Tie line picker for a take of one route across routers. It shows the source and each step with its tie lines. Each tie line shows what it carries now and will carry, and the time since its last change. The picker also lists every Affected destination. J and K or the arrows choose a tie line, H and L or Tab move between steps, Enter sends and Esc cancels. A tap on a wire chooses it. The picker is the only confirm of that take, with the lockout.
