- In the Tie line picker, a user with the Protect right can press P or the shield of a step. After every hop of the take, Sode then protects the upstream destination of that tie line, on its router. The Protect is its own entry in the Group of the take. If it fails, the take stays a success. A Protected tie line shows grey with who set the Protect and when. A Protected tie line that already carries the source stays usable. The API sends `protect` on a tie line in `ties` and gets `protects` in the answer.
