- **Bookmarks** take the place of preset pins. A bookmark opens a preset (through its Confirm), a page, a panel, a filter set, a setting, a Settings button or an outside web address. Keep them for Me, This browser or Everyone: the strip above History shows This browser's list, else Me, else Everyone. Alt+1 to 9 open the first nine, and Alt+Shift+1 to 9 fill the Patch boxes from a preset bookmark. Edit on the strip opens the Bookmarks editor. A list holds 24. A bookmark you cannot use shows No access, and one whose target is gone shows Missing. Renaming a preset or a filter set keeps all its bookmarks, in every scope.
- Sode refuses a bookmark to a `javascript:` or other non-web address, or one that holds a panel key, a setup link or a `#share=` link. Changing Everyone's bookmarks needs a Trusted user or an Administrator.
- Preset pins, the Pinned checkbox and Pin or Unpin in the Presets popup are gone. Old pins are not moved to bookmarks.
