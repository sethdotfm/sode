- Set and clear a Protect on every level of a destination from Sode. Use the shield in the route view (desktop and phone), or `x` and Shift+x in Keyboard mode. The API has `POST` and `DELETE /api/v1/protected/{dst}` with the new `protect` scope. Each one goes through the Confirm and is a History entry, and Undo works on Sode's own Protects. Set Sode's device number in Settings > Routers > Protects first.
- Sode shows the kind of each hold: "Protected by Sode (Ana)", "Protected by 112" or "Router lock by 9". `/api/v1/protected` rows add `kind`, `device` and, for Sode's own, `sodeUser`.
- A new Protect right in Rights, on by default for Trusted users and Administrators. Clearing another user's Protect needs an Administrator or an Approval.
- Settings > Routers > Protects lists every Protect and Router lock, and Clear Sode's Protects clears every Protect with Sode's device number after a PIN recheck.
