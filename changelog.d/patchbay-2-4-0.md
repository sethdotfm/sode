- Sode uses patchbay 2.4.0. On a touch screen, input fields have text of 16px or more, so iOS does not zoom in when you touch a field. Spinners turn more slowly when the computer is set to reduce motion, and do not stop. In Windows High Contrast, selected tabs and rows show an outline, and check boxes and switches show their state. A field with a value out of range turns red only after you leave it, not while you type.
