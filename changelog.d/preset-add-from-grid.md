- Add from Patch Boxes in the preset editor: Take reads Add and adds the patch boxes' route to the preset. It sends nothing to the router. The bar's buttons sit on the accent with no grey box. The title starts at the page content's left edge, or after the lock chip.
- In that mode the Grid popup (Ctrl+E) adds to the preset. Its header says "Add patches to" the preset, and its Take reads Add, with Done beside it. A crosspoint click, or Enter on the cursor, adds that route. A click on a destination name adds the source it has now, one row for each source on the chosen levels. A click on a source name adds every destination it goes to now, one row for each set of levels. Nothing goes to the router, and Esc closes the popup only.
