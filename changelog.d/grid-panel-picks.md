- On a Grid panel, click, Shift-click and Cmd-click names on the Source bar and Destination bar to pick them. A crosspoint press then stages a Diagonal, and the crosspoints it stages show numbered before the press. The header shows "N staged". At most 128 routes can be staged. A press of more than one route opens the confirm, also in Instant mode. The first Esc drops the picks and the second drops the staged routes, also in Kiosk mode.
