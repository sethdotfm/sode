- Each router has its own level count, level offset, level numbering and default levels. Settings > Routers > Router levels edits them for each router after the first. The first router keeps its fields in its own block. The typed levels of a route are levels of the router of its destination, in the desktop Levels box and in the phone's Levels step. On a router with one level, any typed levels mean that one level (the whole signal), and History says "All levels". A router with more levels still refuses a level past its count.
