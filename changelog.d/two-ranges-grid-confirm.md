- Home: a Destination list can have a second range in the name. `MV 1-4 PIP 1-2` gives MV 1 PIP 1, MV 1 PIP 2, MV 2 PIP 1 and so on to MV 4 PIP 2, in that order. The note names the destinations that the router does not have. The limit of 100 destinations counts each name of the list. Three ranges, or a range of numbers before a name, are a normal search.
- Grid panel page: the take Confirm reads and looks as Home's Confirm. The question names each destination by number and name ("Take Source 13 CAM 1 to 4 destinations: 61 MV 1 PIP 1, ...?"), the first 7 and the count of the rest above 8. Each box shows numbered sources and destinations, and what the take replaces shows under "Replacing".
