- The Palette: one search list of the actions, presets, pages, panels, filter sets and settings you can use. Type `>` first in an empty Source or Destination box, or press Ctrl+Shift+P on any page. Type `>>` to search for `>` in the box. A preset still asks for its Confirm, and Shift+Enter fills the Patch boxes from it. Rows outside your Rights or Role are grey and offer Step in or the Approval flow. Home is first on every other page. Your last five picks follow your user, or stay in This browser for a Guest. Firefox on Windows and Linux keeps Ctrl+Shift+P for a private window, so use the Palette character there.
- Settings > This browser and Everyone's defaults have a Palette character setting (default `>`, or no character). Settings warns when router names start with the character.
