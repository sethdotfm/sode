- Compact view hides History on the desktop main page and centres the title, the patch boxes and a smaller Presets row in the window. The Settings and Sode Panels buttons, the Lock bar and the Server and Router status fade out until pointed at or focused. The status shows whenever a link is not up. Alt+Shift+C (Option+Shift+C on a Mac) turns it on or off for This browser, and Settings can set it for This browser, Me or Everyone.
