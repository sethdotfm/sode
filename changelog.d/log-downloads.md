- The Communication Log has Download full log and Download filtered rows in place of Download CSV. Download full log gives a zip with every Log row, oldest first, and the uptime record. Download filtered rows applies the page's filters to the whole log, not only to the loaded rows, and keeps the page's order. The page makes both files from the rows the server sends, so a filter that works on the page also works in the file. A Settings saves row lists its changed fields in the note. Values that the reader's Role cannot see show as "changed". Each download writes a Log row with the count of rows sent.
