- The panel editor makes and edits Grid panels. A new panel has a Buttons or Grid choice in the Panel tab, which you cannot change after the first save. A Grid panel has Sources and Destinations tabs. There you pin, move and remove items. Clear empties the list, and All (follow router) sets it back to the router list. The Panel tab hides the settings that a Grid panel does not use. Items outside your Rights stay in the list and show as a count.
