- A panel has a `type`, `buttons` or `grid`, fixed when it is made. Every existing panel is a Buttons panel. A Grid panel made over the API opens at `/panels/<id>`. Every router source is across the top and every destination down the side, with a level pie on each crosspoint. A click stages a route on the panel's levels, Enter opens the take confirm, and Instant mode sends a click at once. The take confirm uses the confirm lockout and Enter settings.
