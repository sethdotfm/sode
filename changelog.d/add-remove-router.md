- Settings > Routers has Add router and Remove router for an Administrator. A new router gets the first free prefix, a title and its own Router levels block. Sode connects to it at once. Each router after the first has Prefix, Title, Address and Matrix in its row. With two or more routers, the first router's row shows its prefix too. Remove router lists the presets, filter sets and panels it makes Broken before you stage it, and the save asks for your PIN again. Rights lose the references and levels of a removed router.
