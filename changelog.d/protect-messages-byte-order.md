- Sode now reads the router's protect change messages (0x8B, 0x8D, 0x8F) in the Ultrix's byte order. When DashBoard, a panel or another SW-P-08 client changes which levels of a destination are protected, Sode now shows it at once on every level. Before, Sode read these messages wrong until the next resync. It missed new protection, and after a clear it kept levels 2 and up protected.
