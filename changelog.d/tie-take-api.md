- `POST /api/v1/takes` sends a route across routers when the route has `ties`: one tie line for each hop before the last, upstream first. Sode sends the hops upstream first. A hop that two routes of the take share goes one time. If a hop fails, the hops after it do not go. The take is one History Group with an entry for each hop. The entry of the destination asked for keeps the typed source, each hop with its Result and Prev, and the Via chain from before. History names the typed source on that row.
