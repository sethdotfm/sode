- Palette: Protect opens one list of destinations on Home, to protect and to clear. Chips show All destinations, Currently protected or Currently free. Type to filter the list, pick one or more, then confirm them all in one Confirm. Each destination gets its own History row. The row shows only for a user with the Protect right, and only when Sode's device number for Protects is set.
- In the Protect list, the arrows or Ctrl+J/K (Cmd+J/K on a Mac) move the outline. Ctrl+Space picks the outlined row; Space also picks it after a move, or while the find box is empty. A Shift+click picks a range. Enter goes to the Confirm. With nothing picked, it uses the outlined row.
- In the Protect list, each row shows who holds it. A row that Sode cannot change (another system's Protect or a Router lock) shows, but cannot be picked. When the picks hold free and protected destinations, Enter and the main button protect the free ones first, and a second button clears the others. The "N picked" count is a blue chip, as in the Grid popup.
- The Protect Confirm no longer has the line "Nobody can change it until it is cleared".
