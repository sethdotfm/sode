- Grid popup on the main page: Ctrl+e or the Grid button under Take opens a grid of every source and destination in your Rights. Draw a Diagonal or stage routes, then one Confirm sends them as one take, which History shows as one Group. The Results show on the main page. On the phone layout, the Grid button in the header opens it over the whole window.
- Routes staged in the Grid popup stay in a Staged list under the patch boxes after it closes. The main Take sends them, and the main Clear drops them. The crosshair on a row fills the patch boxes. A reload or the Lock drops the list.
- Ctrl+Z in the Grid popup prepares the undo of the last Sode take, a whole Group for a Grid popup take. With no source and destination in the patch boxes, the popup comes back as you left it in this tab.
- In the Grid popup, a shield over a destination name sets or clears Sode's Protect on it, through the Confirm. It shows only where the route view would offer it.
