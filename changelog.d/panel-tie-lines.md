- A panel has a tie line setting, Off or Reserved, in the editor's Panel tab. At Off the panel refuses a route across routers. At Reserved the panel always sends it on its own tie line for each link. The server applies the setting, also to the panel's API client. One tie line can be Reserved by one panel only. Push, templates, Save a copy, checkpoints and Checkpoint sets leave the setting out.
- A panel in Take mode shows the tie line and the Affected destinations before it sends. A Grid panel shows them in its confirm. A Buttons panel shows them after the first Take. A panel in Instant mode sends with no confirm. A refusal says why: no Reserved tie line on a link, two sources through one Reserved tie line, or a Protect of someone else.
- In the Tie line picker a tie line Reserved by a panel says so and sorts with the Protected ones. A person can choose it, and Take asks one more time before it sends. `GET /tielines` names the panel of each Reserve.
