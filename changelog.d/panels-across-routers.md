- Panel pages read names, tally and Protects for each router that the panel uses. A Grid panel can hold sources and destinations of two routers, with each item's router prefix, and the Grid popup on the main page shows every router. The Grid panel editor lists the items of every router with their prefix, and a typed prefix (B.12) finds only that router's items. A route from a panel across two routers is refused at the press until Sode has tie lines.
- `GET /api/v1/protected` takes the `router` parameter, as `/tally` does, and with two or more routers it needs it. `GET /api/v1/panels/{id}/undo` gives the `router` of each route.
