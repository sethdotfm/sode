- API: a tally row whose source a tie line feeds now carries its Via chain, `via` and `origin`, for its own level. It marks a mixed, unknown, nothing or loop chain. When something upstream of a tie line changes, the stream sends the rows of every destination downstream of it. An External on the upstream end of a tie line notes the destinations that it also changes in `also`. Hidden visibility blanks the origins and names that the caller cannot see.
