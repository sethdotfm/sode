- A Grid panel has one cursor for the mouse and the keys. It is a ring on the current crosspoint that grows and shrinks, and it is still when reduced motion is set. It starts at the corner. Arrows and h/j/k/l move it, and Space stages at it. PageUp, PageDown and Alt+arrows move one screen. Home goes to the corner and End to the last row. Ctrl/Cmd+Home and Ctrl/Cmd+End go to the start and end of the row. After a pointer move, a scroll keeps the cursor under the pointer. After a key, a scroll leaves it where the key put it.
