- On a Grid panel, `g` or a right-click on a crosspoint or destination goes to its current source. It uses the lowest level in the Levels box. If a Filter hides that source, the panel says so. `G` or a right-click on a source goes to the next destination that has that source on any level. `u` stages the Undo of this panel instance in place of the staged routes. It asks first when routes are staged, and the Undo always has its confirm, also in Instant mode.
