- The status bar shows one Routers icon at every router count, also with one router. It has no color while every router is up. It is amber while one or more routers are down and one or more are up. It is red while every router is down. While routers are down, the text beside it gives their count, for example "3 of 24 down". The phone header fits at any count.
- A click or a press on the Routers icon opens the router list, which stays open. Lost routers come first. Each router shows its state in words: Connected, Connecting, Down or Never connected. A row opens to show the address, the error and the last 24 hours, with links to Router settings (Administrators) and the Communication Log. From 8 routers the list has a find box. The list replaces the details that showed on hover.
- A router that was not connected one time since Sode started, or since its address changed, shows "Never connected" and "Check the address." in place of "Down".
- With three or more lost routers, the Degraded note is one sentence that names their prefixes, with a Show routers button.
