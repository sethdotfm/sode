The rules this prototype shows

  • One router: its icon, and the text that its Status label setting picks.
  • Two or three routers: one icon for each router, with its prefix and the text that its Status label setting picks.
  • Four or more routers: one Routers icon with a count. A healthy bar shows no color.
  • Each router that is not connected shows by its prefix beside the count: red for down, amber while it connects. The bar shows five, then a count of the rest.
  • Click a prefix, or the Routers icon, to open the list. Lost routers come first. Each row has a link to that router in Settings and to the Communication Log.
  • With three or more lost routers, the Degraded note is one sentence that lists the prefixes.
  • The phone uses the count from two routers. It shows how many routers are lost, for example "5 of 24 lost", and the note under the bar lists their prefixes. A press opens the same list.
